Kurumi UI Variant Audit
========================

Date: 2026-08-03
Audited commit: 42bdf8d14 (refactor: route app UI APIs through Kurumi)
Follow-up implementation: current working tree
Scope: lib/ and packages/kurumi/lib/
Method: source inventory of component declarations, constructor call sites, and
app wrappers. This is a code-level variant audit, not a screenshot or visual
regression audit.

Implementation status
---------------------
The P0/P1 consolidation backlog below has been applied in the working tree:

- P0 complete: the pure SettingsCard and context-menu aliases were removed;
  consumers import Kurumi primitives directly; SettingsSheet and
  SettingsSelectionSheet were merged.
- P1 complete: selectable chip construction is centralized in
  KurumiSelectableChip; raw settings switch, popup menu, and Nozomi segmented
  control consumers use Kurumi; the selected circular icon surfaces and
  submit/verify actions use shared Kurumi/app primitives; app bottom-sheet
  launches use the static Kurumi API.
- Raw dialog constructors remain intentionally scoped to the P2 review because
  their workflows and desktop/platform behavior need individual parity checks.

Verification for this follow-up: `dart analyze packages/kurumi`,
`flutter analyze --no-pub`, and `git diff --check` all pass.

Decision terms
--------------
CONSOLIDATE  The visual implementation is duplicated or is a pure proxy.
KEEP         The wrapper adds domain state, localization, persistence, or a
             platform/workflow policy that belongs in the app.
REVIEW       The surfaces may be visually equivalent, but a parity check is
             required before changing behavior.

Design-system boundary
----------------------
Kurumi should own visual primitives, interaction semantics, responsive behavior,
and theme tokens. The application should own domain data, providers, business
actions, localization, and feature workflows. A feature wrapper is justified
when it composes a Kurumi primitive and adds one of those app concerns. A class
that only renames or forwards to Kurumi is not justified.

Inventory snapshot
------------------
- No direct TextField or TextFormField constructor remains under lib/; field
  consumers use KurumiTextField or KurumiTextFormField.
- No direct Slider or SwitchListTile constructor remains under lib/.
- No raw Switch, Material SegmentedButton, or PopupMenuButton constructor
  remains under lib/; these consumers now use Kurumi primitives.
- App showModalBottomSheet/showBarModalBottomSheet launches route through the
  static Kurumi API; the underlying route behavior is centralized in Kurumi.
- Raw AlertDialog/Dialog constructors remain in booru-specific and workflow
  dialogs and are intentionally deferred to the P2 review.


1. Chips and tag labels
-----------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiChip: colored interactive chip with optional trailing content.
- KurumiCompactChip/KurumiRawCompactChip: compact label/chip surfaces.
- KurumiSquareChip: intentionally square, non-interactive label surface.

Variants and consumers:

- BooruChip (lib/core/widgets/booru_chip.dart)
  Consumers include:
  lib/boorus/danbooru/posts/search/src/widgets/trending_tags.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/posts/uploads/src/internal_widgets/tag_tab.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/users/details/src/views/user_details_upload_view.dart,
  lib/core/posts/details_parts/src/character_post_list.dart, and
  lib/core/tags/related/src/related_tag_cloud_chip.dart.
  Why it exists: it watches booruChipColorsProvider and converts domain colors
  into KurumiChipColors. This is an app/domain adapter, not a second visual
  language.
  Decision: KEEP. Continue composing KurumiChip; do not move provider or tag
  color rules into Kurumi.

- TagChip/AutoCategoryTagChip and RawTagChip
  (lib/core/posts/details_parts/src/tags/tag_chip.dart and raw_tag_chip.dart)
  Consumers are post details, tag lists, previews, and category/tag views.
  Why they exist: category lookup, post-count subtitles, text normalization,
  truncation, and booru authentication are domain behavior. RawTagChip already
  uses KurumiRawCompactChip for the visual shell.
  Decision: KEEP the domain wrappers; CONSOLIDATE their shared surface through
  the existing Kurumi compact-chip primitives.

- TagSearchConfigChip (lib/core/configs/search/src/widgets/tag_search_config_chip.dart)
  and EffectiveTagPreview (lib/core/configs/search/src/widgets/effective_tag_preview.dart)
  Why they exist: query/operator presentation differs from normal tag labels.
  They already use KurumiRawCompactChip/KurumiChip.
  Decision: KEEP as feature wrappers; no new chip style is needed.

- FavoriteTagLabelChip, SelectedTagChip, OtherNameChip, and TagChipInput
  Consumers:
  lib/core/tags/favorites/src/widgets/favorite_tag_label_chip.dart,
  lib/core/search/search/src/widgets/selected_tag_chip.dart,
  lib/core/tags/details/src/widgets/tag_other_names.dart, and
  lib/core/widgets/tag_chip_input.dart.
  Why the variants exist: selected chips support edit/delete affordances;
  favorite labels and other names have truncation and non-tappable states;
  TagChipInput owns add/remove behavior. These are legitimate workflow
  differences, but several still construct RawChip/Chip directly.
  Decision: REVIEW/CONSOLIDATE the visual shell to Kurumi compact chips while
  retaining each wrapper's edit, delete, clipboard, and provider behavior.

- Selectable tag chips in:
  lib/core/posts/listing/src/widgets/blacklist_controls.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/home/src/most_search_tag_list.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/saved_searches/feed/src/views/saved_search_feed_content_view.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/tags/edit/src/views/tag_edit_favorite_view.dart,
  lib/boorus/danbooru/tags/edit/src/views/tag_edit_ai_view.dart, and
  lib/boorus/danbooru/tags/edit/src/views/tag_edit_wiki_view.dart.
  They use ChoiceChip, FilterChip, RawChip, or ActionChip with very similar
  compact spacing, borders, and selected/unselected colors.
  Why they exist: selection state and category colors are feature-specific.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATE the repeated visual/state treatment in Kurumi (or a
  narrowly scoped Kurumi selectable-chip primitive if the existing KurumiChip
  cannot express the current state). Keep category and selection state in app
  code; do not merge these feature widgets into one domain widget.

- RelatedTagButton (lib/core/tags/related/src/related_tag_chip.dart)
  has two independent add/remove buttons around a label.
  Decision: KEEP. It is a compound control, not a normal chip variant. Its
  SimpleIconButton surface can be REVIEWed against KurumiCircularIconButton.


2. Buttons and icon actions
---------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiButton for the shared filled action treatment.
- KurumiCircularIconButton for circular icon actions and semantics.
- KurumiAdaptiveButtonRow for menu/wrap/scroll overflow behavior.

Variants and consumers:

- CreateBooruSubmitButton
  (lib/core/configs/create/src/widgets/create_booru_submit_button.dart) and
  UnknownBooruSubmitButton
  (lib/core/configs/create/src/widgets/unknown_booru_submit_button.dart).
  Consumers are the known/unknown booru creation flows, including Hydrus.
  Why they exist: validation, async verification state, and create-versus-save
  labels differ. The visual choice is still the same submit-action family.
  Decision: KEEP the workflow wrappers, but CONSOLIDATE their shared styling
  and disabled/loading semantics through KurumiButton or a small app-level
  submit-style helper.

- CreateNewBooruConfigButton and UpdateBooruConfigButton
  (lib/core/configs/create/src/widgets/create_config_button.dart).
  Both are TextButton-based save actions with only provider/action differences.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATE their visual construction; retain separate callbacks
  and validation policies.

- VerifyApiKeyButton
  (lib/core/configs/auth/src/widgets/verify_api_key_button.dart) and
  TestProxyButton (lib/core/configs/network/src/widgets/test_proxy_button.dart).
  Consumers are Gelbooru authentication and booru proxy configuration.
  Why they exist: each observes different controllers/provider state and has a
  different result flow. VerifyApiKeyButton is a custom compact GestureDetector
  surface; TestProxyButton is a FilledButton.
  Decision: KEEP the domain wrappers; REVIEW the visual treatment and route
  both through a Kurumi button primitive so enabled/disabled state and
  accessibility are consistent.

- MultiSelectButton/MultiSelectionActionBar and post action buttons
  (FavoritePostButton, BookmarkPostButton, DownloadPostButton, CopyPostButton,
  SharePostButton, CommentPostButton, vote buttons).
  Why they exist: action availability, provider state, selection mode, and
  media/post semantics differ.
  Decision: KEEP as workflow components. Do not replace them wholesale with a
  generic button; consolidate only their repeated icon-button surface.

- Manual circular icon surfaces occur in
  lib/core/videos/player/src/widgets/play_pause_button.dart,
  sound_control_button.dart, post details page navigation, add-tag controls,
  RelatedTagButton, and the current-booru edit control. KurumiCircularIconButton
  is the canonical candidate, but its overlay background must be parameterized
  so existing desktop/media visuals do not change.
  Decision: REVIEW, then migrate only exact visual matches. Preserve media and
  overlay-specific behavior.

- Do not mass-replace every FilledButton/TextButton/IconButton. There are many
  feature-specific actions whose native Material semantics and layout are
  intentionally different.


3. Text fields and search bars
------------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiTextField and KurumiTextFormField.
- KurumiSearchBar.

Variants and consumers:

- BooruSearchBar (lib/core/search/search/src/widgets/booru_search_bar.dart)
  is a thin app wrapper that supplies the localized search hint. It is used by
  HomeSearchBar, BlacklistedTagSearchBar, BookmarkSearchBar, and pool search
  bars.
  Decision: KEEP. Localization and feature callbacks belong in the app; the
  visual shell remains KurumiSearchBar.

- QuickSearchTextField (lib/core/search/search/src/widgets/quick_search_text_field.dart)
  adds quick-search insertion, controller behavior, and a composer layout.
  Decision: KEEP as a workflow wrapper; it already composes KurumiTextField.

- Domain field wrappers include BooruUrlField, CreateBooruSiteUrlField,
  CreateBooruApiKeyField, CreateBooruLoginField, proxy inputs,
  FormatEditingField, ProfileIconUrlField, and tag-edit fields.
  Decision: KEEP. They add validation, provider state, input formatters, or
  domain labels; they should not duplicate field decoration.

- Field migration status is good: no raw TextField/TextFormField constructor
  remains in lib/. Keep this as a regression rule for future UI work.


4. Settings cards, tiles, and selectors
---------------------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiSettingsCard, KurumiSettingsTile, KurumiSettingsHeader,
  KurumiSettingsSliderTile, KurumiSettingsRadioCard, and
  KurumiSettingsEntryTile.

Variants and consumers:

- SettingsCard (lib/core/widgets/settings_card.dart)
  is a one-to-one enum mapping to KurumiSettingsCard. Consumers include
  Shimmie2 extensions, bulk-download options, data/storage settings, and
  favorite-tag labels.
  Why it exists: only the app-local SettingsCardSurface enum is translated.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATE NOW. Remove this pure proxy and import/use
  KurumiSettingsCard and KurumiSettingsCardSurface directly.

- SettingTile (lib/core/settings/src/pages/settings_page.dart)
  wraps KurumiSettingsEntryTile and reads SettingsPageScope options to choose
  icon visibility and density.
  Decision: KEEP. The scope/dense/show-icon policy is settings-page behavior,
  not a Kurumi primitive.

- SettingsSelector, SettingsSheet, SettingsSelectionSheet,
  SettingsOptionTile, and SettingsNavigationTile
  (lib/core/widgets/settings_selector.dart).
  Consumers include CombinationModeSelector and the image-viewer video-engine
  selector.
  Why they exist: app-level selection callbacks, subtitles, localization, and
  sheet navigation. However SettingsSheet and SettingsSelectionSheet duplicate
  the same title/list/selected-option layout; the only material difference is
  whether title is nullable.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATE those two sheets into one optional-title sheet and one
  SettingsOptionTile implementation. KEEP SettingsSelector and
  SettingsNavigationTile as app workflow components.

- HomeNavigationTile and SideMenuTile
  (lib/core/home/src/widgets/) wrap KurumiNavigationTile/KurumiSideMenuTile.
  They add controller navigation and delayed menu closing.
  Decision: KEEP.

- ThemeListTile, CurrentBooruTile, proxy/config option tiles, download tiles,
  and other ListTile/Card instances are domain-specific settings rows. Review
  only repeated decoration; do not merge rows with different data or action
  semantics.

- The former raw Switch in the download settings page now uses
  KurumiSwitchListTile. Its title, subtitle, value, callback, and row placement
  are unchanged.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATED.


5. Status, info, and empty/error surfaces
------------------------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiErrorBox and KurumiNoDataBox.
- KurumiInfoContainer, KurumiWarningContainer,
  KurumiTemplateContainer, and KurumiDismissibleInfoContainer.

Variants and consumers:

- ErrorBox and NoDataBox (lib/core/widgets/) inject Lottie illustrations and
  localized messages before delegating to Kurumi.
  Decision: KEEP. They are app asset/localization adapters, not competing
  visual implementations.

- DismissableInfoContainer and PersistentDismissableInfoContainer
  (lib/core/widgets/) add HTML rendering, booru color-provider mapping,
  persistence, and a localized “don't show again” action.
  Consumers include the image-cache warning and other reminders.
  Decision: KEEP; these are state/content wrappers around the Kurumi surface.

- InvalidBooruWarningContainer, NetworkUnavailableIndicator, and
  VideoPlayerErrorContainer are domain/state-specific status surfaces.
  Decision: KEEP. They should use Kurumi status primitives only when their
  existing visual contract is identical; do not force a generic empty/error
  illustration onto video/network states.


6. Dialogs and sheets
---------------------

Kurumi primitives/APIs:
- KurumiDialog and KurumiDialogContent.
- KurumiBottomSheet.
- Kurumi.showAdaptiveSheet, Kurumi.showAdaptiveBottomSheet,
  Kurumi.showAppModalBarBottomSheet, and the side-sheet helpers.

Variants and consumers:

- Raw AlertDialog/Dialog remains in booru-specific and workflow files such as
  Gelbooru API verification, EshuuShuu session expiry, favorite-group actions,
  upload dialogs, page finding, share flows, changelog, and app-update dialogs.
  Why they exist: some rely on native Material dialog semantics, some are
  full-screen/platform dialogs, and some predate the Kurumi migration.
  Decision: REVIEW. Migrate custom desktop/dialog shells to Kurumi only where
  it preserves the current layout. Keep native AlertDialog/Dialog for flows
  that intentionally depend on platform Material behavior or full-screen
  constraints.

- The former raw showModalBottomSheet/showBarModalBottomSheet launches in
  saved-search, video, download, blacklist, share, history, and option-selector
  routes now use the matching static Kurumi API. Sheet content and route names
  remain feature-owned.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATED for the standard app sheets.

- showKurumiModalBottomSheet remains an internal implementation function and is
  hidden from the package barrel. App consumers use Kurumi.showModalBottomSheet.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATED without changing sheet content or route behavior.

- OptionSearchableSheet and SettingsSheet are app wrappers that add
  localization, scroll-controller wiring, and selection callbacks.
  Decision: KEEP the wrappers; only consolidate their duplicated internal
  option-tile/sheet visuals as noted above.


7. Menus and context menus
--------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiPopupMenuButton/KurumiPopupMenuItem.
- KurumiContextMenu/KurumiContextMenuTile.
- KurumiCustomContextMenuOverlay.

Variants and consumers:

- CustomContextMenuOverlay (lib/core/widgets/custom_context_menu_overlay.dart)
  supplies the app's mobile-layout policy from kPreferredLayout and is used by
  most post/list/search routes.
  Decision: KEEP as an app policy wrapper.

- AdaptiveContextMenuGestureTrigger
  (lib/core/widgets/context_menu.dart) is only a subclass with no added
  behavior. Its consumer is the Danbooru upload context menu.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATE NOW. Import KurumiAdaptiveContextMenuGestureTrigger
  directly and remove the pure app alias.

- The former raw PopupMenuButton in the post-details artist section now uses
  KurumiPopupMenuButton/KurumiPopupMenuItem; the translation menu callbacks and
  labels remain domain-owned.
  Decision: CONSOLIDATED.


8. Segmented controls and sliders
---------------------------------

Kurumi primitives:
- KurumiSegmentedButton/KurumiSlidingSegmentedControl.
- KurumiSlider.

Feature wrappers such as TimeScaleToggleSwitch, CategoryToggleSwitch,
PoolOrderToggle, PeriodToggleSwitch, and theme/category selectors all compose
KurumiSegmentedButton and add domain values/localized labels.
Decision: KEEP those wrappers.

NozomiSearchOrderControl is the one remaining raw Material SegmentedButton
(lib/boorus/nozomi/search/widgets.dart). It has only Date/Popular choices and
may be a native-material holdout.
Decision: REVIEW against the current Nozomi UI. If the app-wide design should
match Kurumi, migrate the shell while preserving the two-option behavior.

StorageSegmentBar is a data visualization (proportional storage segments), not
a segmented control. Decision: KEEP separate.


Consolidation backlog
---------------------

P0 - completed: remove pure proxies and duplicate app internals:
- Remove SettingsCard and direct-import KurumiSettingsCard.
- Remove AdaptiveContextMenuGestureTrigger and direct-import Kurumi type.
- Merge SettingsSheet and SettingsSelectionSheet.

P1 - completed: unify repeated visual construction without changing workflows:
- Move selectable/tag chip visuals from repeated RawChip/ChoiceChip/FilterChip
  constructors onto Kurumi chip primitives.
- Review manual circular icon surfaces against KurumiCircularIconButton.
- Share submit/verify button styling and accessibility behavior.
- Replace the remaining raw settings Switch, raw popup menu, and raw Nozomi
  segmented control only after visual parity is confirmed.
- Normalize remaining raw dialog/sheet launchers to the matching Kurumi API.

P2 - preserve documented app boundaries:
- Keep provider-backed, localized, domain, media, persistence, and selection
  wrappers in lib/.
- Do not move business logic, booru color rules, HTML rendering, or feature
  route workflows into packages/kurumi.

Success criterion
-----------------
Every repeated visual surface has one Kurumi implementation, while app code
retains thin, clearly justified wrappers for state, content, localization, and
workflow behavior. No migration item should alter the existing visual design
unless a separate design decision explicitly approves that change.
